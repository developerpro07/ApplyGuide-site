About ApplyGuide

ApplyGuide is built by Dheeraj Sharma for families navigating college applications. Instead of guessing what matters most, you get a living checklist of sourced requirements and a short plan of what to finish in the next 7–14 days. We deliberately do not show admission probabilities or “chancing.”

Features

Document extraction

Upload transcript and activities text — AI (or a local mock) extracts structured facts.

Readiness snapshot

See complete, in-progress, and missing items across your college list.

Ranked next actions

Tasks scored by deadline urgency, impact, effort, and dependency — with reasons.

Essay flags

Structural review flags only. No rewrites. No odds.

Narrative check

Catch inconsistencies between essays, activities, and profile (Pro).

Sourced requirements

Every requirement carries a source URL and checked date, or is marked unverified.

How it works

  1. Profile & colleges — Add GPA/grade/major and your college list.
  2. Documents — Paste transcript/activities/essay text for extraction.
  3. Diagnosis — Get readiness %, missing items, and a ranked plan.
  4. Complete & reprioritize — Finish tasks; Pro users get an updated plan.
